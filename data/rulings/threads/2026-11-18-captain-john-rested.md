Thread: Captain John interaction while rested
Date: 2026-11-18
Cards: OP17-044
Rules: 7-1-1-1

[PLAYER]
Scenario: Captain John (OP17-044) is rested and my opponent declares an attack into Leader despite Captain John's effect ("If your Leader's type includes 'Rocks Pirates' and this Character is rested, your opponent cannot attack any card other than the Character [Captain John]").
Question: Because the attack was an illegal action, will the game be reversed to before my opponent declares the attack, or will they simply lose the attack and nothing happens?

[JUDGE]
Because the player made an invalid attack, you would have them choose a valid target for the attack.

[PLAYER]
Follow-up: Are we sure this is the correct way to solve this issue? It seems wrong to force the attack onto a legal target instead of rewinding the attack.

[JUDGE T1]
Context matters, which is why judge investigations should always be done. Both rulings (choosing a legal target vs. rewinding the attack) can be issued under different circumstances.

[JUDGE]
Agree with above (highly simplified, and circumstances may differ based on investigation):
- Scenario A: Player rests a card for an attack, declares an attack, scans the board, and chooses Leader -> selecting a valid target may apply.
- Scenario B: Player declares "I attack Leader with Luffy" and rests the card for the attack -> rewinding may apply.
These are not the same and would have two different rulings depending on the circumstances established during the judge investigation.
